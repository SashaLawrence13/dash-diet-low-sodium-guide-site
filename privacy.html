DASH Diet: Low Sodium Guide privacy policy

DASH Diet: Low Sodium Guide is made by Sasha Lawrence. Last updated 28 October 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kit in the app. Everything you log stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The foods you log, your blood pressure readings, your plan settings and the cuisines you picked are stored in the app's own database on your device. If you turn on iCloud backup for your phone, Apple may include that database in your device backup under your own iCloud account. I never see it.

HealthKit

If you allow it, the app reads blood pressure readings from Apple Health and writes the readings you enter in the app back to Apple Health. It never reads anything else and never writes anything else. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone and never leaves your device except through the report you choose to share yourself. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open database, to look up the product's sodium. No other information is sent. If you never scan a barcode, the app never connects to the internet at all.

Reports

The 30-day report is a PDF built on your phone. It goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app under Settings and at this page with a new date.
